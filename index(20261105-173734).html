How to start

  1. Add a supplier you find online or offline.
  2. Add that supplier's actual stocklot product and specifications.
  3. Add buyer companies you discover.
  4. Open Matching to see possible product–buyer matches.
  5. Prepare an email draft and check every fact before sending.
