Business workflow

  1. Search Bangladesh for garment stocklots and surplus offers.
  2. Save useful findings in Supplier Product Leads.
  3. Contact suppliers and verify actual stock, price, quantity and terms.
  4. Update the supplier lead status as discussions progress.
  5. After agreement and permission, upload the offer to Products.
  6. The app will automatically search for international buyer prospects after a product is saved.
  7. Review the buyer assessments and prepare emails for your approval.

Discovered supplier offers are not verified inventory. Buyer prospects are not confirmed purchasers.
