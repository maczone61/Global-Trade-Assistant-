How to use the business workflow

  1. Search for garment stocklots and surplus offers using Discover suppliers.
  2. Review the public evidence and save useful findings in Supplier Product Leads.
  3. Contact the supplier yourself and verify the actual stock, price, quantity and terms.
  4. Update the lead status as discussions progress.
  5. Only after reaching an agreement, change its status to Agreement reached and upload it to Products.
  6. Use Discover buyers to research international companies for your active products.
  7. Review buyer evidence, matching signals and email drafts before taking action.

Discovered supplier offers are not verified inventory. A discovered company is not automatically a supplier, and a buyer prospect is not a confirmed buyer.

Bangladesh supplier and stocklot discovery

Search public websites for Bangladesh garment stocklots, export surplus, cancelled orders, factory excess stock and wholesale clothing offers. The search covers Bangladesh nationwide; it is not restricted to a particular district.

Search findings are unverified leads, not confirmed available inventory. You must contact suppliers and verify the products before offering them to buyers.

The search uses the connected Cloudflare Worker and Tavily. The current Worker performs a maximum of three public searches per request. Searches may not find every offer on the internet.
